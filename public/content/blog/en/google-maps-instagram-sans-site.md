Google Maps and Instagram are useful ways to be discovered and stay connected with a community. But being present on these platforms does not offer the same control or depth as an official website.

## What these platforms do well

A Google Business Profile can help a business appear in local searches, show its opening hours and guide people to its premises. Instagram can convey its atmosphere, share news and show day-to-day activity.

These tools therefore have a place in a broader online presence. The point is not to abandon them, but to understand what they cannot replace.

## You depend on rules you do not control

A platform can change its algorithm, interface or the information it chooses to highlight. An account can be restricted, compromised or suspended. An important post is soon buried in the feed.

Your domain, by contrast, remains a stable point of reference. It can appear on printed materials, be added to an email signature or be shared directly without depending on a particular publishing format.

> Platforms are entry points. Your website is the destination you control.

## A listing or profile lacks depth

A business often offers several services, with different details, areas covered and answers depending on the customer’s needs. A local listing cannot explain everything, while a social network favours short, time-sensitive content.

A website can give each service a clear, dedicated page. Visitors can understand the approach, check useful information and prepare their enquiry. This level of detail also helps search engines and AI assistants identify what the business does.

## Presenting every service clearly

On a website, you control how the information is organised:

- a clear main message on the homepage;
- an overview of what the business offers;
- a page for each important service;
- answers to frequently asked questions;
- an introduction to the team;
- a form suited to the enquiry.

Every piece of information has a stable URL. It can be shared directly and found through a specific search.

## The role of an official domain

A domain that matches the business name helps build trust. It enables the business to use a professional email address and becomes the reference point linked from every external profile.

This consistency also reduces ambiguity. Customers can more easily distinguish official information from an outdated directory entry or an account with a similar name.

## The right combination

A website, Google Maps and Instagram are not competing tools. They serve different but complementary purposes. The local listing supports nearby discovery, the social network maintains a connection and the website explains, reassures and makes contact easier.

A sound approach is to make the website the central source, then keep the details consistent across every platform: the same name, business activity, address and contact information.
