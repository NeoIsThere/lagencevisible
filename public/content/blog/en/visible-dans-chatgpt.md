One question comes up increasingly often: how can I get ChatGPT to mention my business? There is no magic switch or method that guarantees a mention. There are, however, useful foundations that can make a business easier to find and understand.

## Start with an accessible website

Your website should be easy to access, without unnecessary barriers. Important pages should be public, fast and connected through clear, logical navigation. Any search engines you allow should be able to crawl them.

A visually impressive page made up entirely of images—or one that reveals its content only after several interactions—gives crawlers little information to work with. Essential copy should be clearly available as text on the page.

## Describe your services accurately

A catch-all phrase such as ‘solutions for all your projects’ does not explain what your business does. Name your services using the words customers would use. Explain the need they address, how you work and the area you cover.

A dedicated page for each important service provides more context than a long list on the homepage. Each page can answer specific questions and relate to a particular search.

## Answer real questions

People use AI assistants conversationally. Content that gives clear answers to specific questions is therefore particularly useful:

- what does the service involve?
- which towns or cities do you cover?
- which documents should customers prepare?
- what are the key stages?
- how should someone choose between two options?

An FAQ can provide brief answers. Articles can explore topics that need more detailed explanations.

## Publish original, consistent content

Copying generic text helps neither your customers nor search engines. Your website should reflect what genuinely sets your business apart: its experience, approach, decisions and knowledge of its field.

Check that the information is consistent too. The name, address, contact details and main description should remain the same on the website, local listing and professional profiles.

## Add structured data

Structured data is information added to a page’s code to explain more precisely what its content represents. It can indicate that an element describes a business, service, person, article or frequently asked question.

It can never replace clear, visible content. Instead, it supports that content by reducing ambiguity. A sitemap and descriptive page titles complete this structure.

## What no one can guarantee

No one can guarantee that a business will appear in a particular answer or control the wording generated by an external assistant. The sources consulted, models and features all change.

> The realistic goal is to build the best possible official source, not to promise an answer you cannot control.

This work remains useful beyond AI assistants. A clear website also helps Google, other search engines and, above all, people who want to assess your credibility before getting in touch.
